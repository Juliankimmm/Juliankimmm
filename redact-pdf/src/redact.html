⬛ PDF Redactor

Everything happens on this computer. The PDF is never uploaded anywhere.

1 Open a PDF

Drop a PDF here, or click to choose one

2 What to black out

Type one item per line: names, addresses, account numbers, any words. Matches are blacked out on the right as you type.

Also find automatically:

    3 Check, then save

    On the pages: drag to black out anything else (signatures, pictures, scanned text). Click a black box to keep that one spot visible; click it again to black it out again. Click a box you drew to remove it.

    Why the result can’t be undone: many tools only lay a black box over the words, and the words can still be copied out from underneath. This tool rebuilds every page as a flat picture with the black boxes burned in. The saved PDF has no text, fonts, form fields, comments, bookmarks or hidden data left in it, so there is nothing to recover. After saving, the tool reopens the new file and checks this.

    Because the pages become pictures, text in the saved file can’t be selected or searched. Keep the original in a safe place.

    The tool only finds typed text. Words that are part of a scanned image must be covered by dragging a box over them.
